Vibes

A vibe is a ready-made direction for how your game looks and feels: colours, light, a drawing style and a mood. Any vibe works on any starter game.

Click a card to see it large. When you like one, tell your agent: "use the ... vibe".

The mood pictures were made by an AI image model: they show a feeling to aim for, and your game is never judged on matching them. The pictures marked "the kit's own game" are real screenshots of a starter game with only the vibe's colours, light and drawing style on: the same shapes, no new models.
