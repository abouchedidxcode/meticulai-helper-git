# About the pictures in `vibes/*/frames/`

**They are AI-generated mood pictures.** Nobody drew or photographed them. They were made with image and video models
through fal.ai in November 2026; each vibe's `vibe.json` names the model for every frame, and the prompt where one
was written for this gallery. Pictures from Google's image model carry Google's invisible SynthID mark, which says a
picture is AI-generated.

**How you may use them.** As far as we are able to, we release these pictures under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): copy, change and share them for any purpose, with no
credit needed. They are not covered by the kit's MIT licence or by the CC BY licence of the written guides.

**What we cannot promise.** In many countries a picture made by an AI model has no copyright owner at all, so there
may be nothing for us to license; that is why this is a release and not a licence with conditions. We also cannot
promise that a generated picture resembles nothing that already exists. We looked at every frame and removed any that
showed text, logos or anything we recognised from an existing game, film or brand. If you see one we missed, please
open an issue and we will take it out.

**What they are for.** Choosing a feeling for your game. They are not art for your game: make your own characters and
places. And they are not a target: the kit never judges a game on matching a frame (see [README.md](README.md)).

The `vibe.json` files themselves (palettes, descriptions, lists) are part of the kit and follow the kit's licences.
