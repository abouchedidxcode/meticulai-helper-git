## How to search and download

- Open https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc (archive of yearly bundles, 2015 onward; the 2026 bundle is on gdc.sonniss.com).
- Pick one part whose contents match the need (each part lists its libraries) and let the user download it by hand.
- Search the unzipped folder locally (e.g. `find . -iname "*whoosh*"`), copy 2-10 files into `assets/audio/`, convert to ogg.

## Good for / not for

- Good for: Professional-grade sound effects (weapons, foley, ambience, UI, creatures, vehicles) when you want quality without credits.
- Not for: Quick agent downloads (parts are several GB each); any AI training; re-sharing the raw sounds.

## Gotchas

- Huge downloads: have the user download one bundle part by hand, then copy only the few files you need into the game. Do not mass-download.
- Not a CC licence. Allowed: use and modify in commercial games, no attribution. Forbidden: distributing the files "as sound effects" (no sound packs, no public asset repo of the raw files).
- AI/ML training with these sounds is explicitly prohibited.
- Files are large 24-bit/96 kHz wavs: trim and convert to ogg/mp3 before shipping.
- Keep the bundle licence PDF/text with your project records.
