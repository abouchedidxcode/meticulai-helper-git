## How to search and download

- Open the generator, choose body, hair, clothes, weapon; use the licence filter to limit layers to the licences you accept.
- Download the PNG sheet and the credits file (csv or txt) together; commit both.

## Good for / not for

- Good for: Top-down RPG characters with many outfits and full animation sets, made without drawing.
- Not for: Games that cannot ship a credits list, or that want closed/DRM-protected assets.

## Gotchas

- Each layer has its own licence: CC0, CC BY, OGA-BY, CC BY-SA 4.0 or GPL 3. The README says the most restrictive is CC BY-SA and that commercial games are allowed.
- SHARE-ALIKE: if the sheet includes CC BY-SA or GPL layers, any modified version of that art must be released under the same licence. The game code is not affected, but the sprite sheet is.
- CC BY / CC BY-SA layers forbid DRM/"technical measures" on the art; fine for a plain browser game, a problem for some app stores.
- Credit every author: download the credits (csv/txt) from the generator for your exact layers and ship it, visible from the credits screen.
- Tick only CC0 / OGA-BY layers in the licence filter if you want the least obligations.
