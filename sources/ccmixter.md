# ccMixter (dig.ccmixter.org)

Instrumental remixes and loops (electronic, hip-hop, ambient, cinematic) where you can credit the artist.

Site: https://dig.ccmixter.org

## Licence

**mixed**

> It’s free to use if you give us credit

Source: https://ccmixter.org/ (checked 2026-11-14)

- Credit required: Yes. Credit line: `"{title}" by {author} ({url}), {license}`
- Commercial use OK: Yes (only for items whose own licence allows it; see gotchas)
- AI-generated content: No

## What is there

- Kinds: music
- Styles: any
- Approx count: not stated
- Formats: mp3
- Access: site. no key; dig.ccmixter.org has a "free for commercial use" filter, an "instrumental only" checkbox and a "music for games" featured list. Docs: https://ccmixter.org/

## How to search and download

- Open https://dig.ccmixter.org, use the filters panel: tick "free for commercial use" and, for game background music, "instrumental only".
- Open a track, confirm the licence (CC BY or CC0), download the mp3, record the attribution text the page gives.

## Good for / not for

- Good for: Instrumental remixes and loops (electronic, hip-hop, ambient, cinematic) where you can credit the artist.
- Not for: Anything you did not filter with "free for commercial use"; the site default mixes in NonCommercial tracks.

## Gotchas

- Licence is per track and many are CC BY-NC: always switch on the "free for commercial use" filter and re-check the licence on the track page.
- Credit is required (CC BY family). Remixes may also list the sources they sample: credit what the track page asks for.
- The ccMixter site text itself is CC BY-NC; that applies to the website, not to the music licence of each track.
