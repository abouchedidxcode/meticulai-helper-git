## How to search and download

- Open https://soundimage.org and pick a genre page from the menu (e.g. "Fantasy 1", "Puzzle Music", "Sci-Fi").
- Each track has a preview and an mp3 download link; download by hand (the site rejects scripted requests).
- Add "Music by Eric Matyas, www.soundimage.org" to the in-game credits.
