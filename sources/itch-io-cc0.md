## How to search and download

- Listing of assets whose creator set the CC0 licence: https://itch.io/game-assets/assets-cc0 (sorted by popularity; `.xml` for RSS).
- Free + tag: https://itch.io/game-assets/free/tag-cc0 , https://itch.io/game-assets/free/tag-pixel-art , https://itch.io/game-assets/free/tag-low-poly
- Stacked filters trigger a bot check: send the link to the user rather than fetching it.
- On the page, read "License" in "More information" and the description before downloading.

## Good for / not for

- Good for: Pixel-art packs, tilesets, character sprites, small 3D packs, music packs from indie artists.
- Not for: Anything without a clear licence line on its page.

## Gotchas

- Read the page: "free" on itch does not mean CC0. Some say "free for commercial use, no redistribution" or "credit required"; some are NC.
- The tag "cc0" is set by uploaders and is sometimes wrong; prefer the licence field (assets-cc0 listing) and the text on the page.
- Mixed artists: pick one artist or one pack family to keep the look coherent.
