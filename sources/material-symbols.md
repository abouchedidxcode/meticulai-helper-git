## How to search and download

- Browse https://fonts.google.com/icons and download the SVG, or `curl -s https://api.iconify.design/material-symbols/pause-rounded.svg -o pause.svg`.

## Good for / not for

- Good for: Very large, familiar UI icon set (outlined/rounded/sharp, fill and weight axes).
- Not for: A distinctive game look (instantly reads as Android/Google UI).
