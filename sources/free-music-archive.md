# Free Music Archive (FMA)

Songs with vocals or a distinct artist style (rock, electronic, hip-hop, ambient) when you can credit.

Site: https://freemusicarchive.org

## Licence

**mixed**

> Find the specific CC licence conditions on the song page of the FMA artist.

Source: https://freemusicarchive.org/License_Guide (checked 2026-11-14)

- Credit required: Yes. Credit line: `"{title}" by {author} ({url}), {license}`
- Commercial use OK: Yes (only for items whose own licence allows it; see gotchas)
- AI-generated content: No

## What is there

- Kinds: music
- Styles: any
- Approx count: not stated
- Formats: mp3
- Access: site. per-track licence chosen by the artist; downloads may need a free account (user does it). Docs: https://freemusicarchive.org/License_Guide

## How to search and download

- Search or browse by genre on https://freemusicarchive.org, then open each candidate track page and read its licence badge.
- Keep only CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA. Download the mp3 (user may need to sign in).
- Record title, artist, URL and licence in the credits file immediately.

## Good for / not for

- Good for: Songs with vocals or a distinct artist style (rock, electronic, hip-hop, ambient) when you can credit.
- Not for: Fast agent-driven sourcing: most tracks are NonCommercial or NoDerivatives, so every pick needs a manual licence check.

## Gotchas

- Licence is per track. Only CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA are usable in a commercial game. Skip anything with NC or ND.
- Retired "FMA-Limited License: Download Only" tracks are personal-listening only: never use them.
- FMA itself does not grant licences; the artist does. Screenshot or save the track page licence at download time.
- Credit must include title, artist, source and licence.
