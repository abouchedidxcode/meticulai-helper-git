## How to fill an entry

- **For:** the one or two things this reference is here to show (the light, the camera height, how a hit feels).
- **Ignore:** what NOT to take from it (its characters, its logo, its UI, its colours if only the camera matters).
- **Notes:** anything the user said about it, in their words.

- **For:** low warm sun from one side, long shadows, the sea darker than the sky.
- **Ignore:** the boats and the town (we have neither); the heavy film grain.
- **Notes:** the user: "this is the mood, quiet and a bit lonely".
