# Credits for the blocks

Every block here is someone else's work, shared openly. Thank you to all of them.

**Thank you to Patch ([patch.gg](https://patch.gg))** for the blocks they made and published openly under MIT and CC0, and
for crediting every author they built on so carefully that this page was easy to write. The blocks in `blocks/vendor/`
marked "Patch" or "blendi" are theirs.

If your game uses a block, `node tools/blocks/add.mjs <id>` adds its credit line to `my-game/CREDITS.md`. Keep each
block's `LICENSE` and credits files with any copy. "CC-BY" means you must name the author in your game's credits.

Checked 2026-11-14.
